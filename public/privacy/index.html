Privacy

Your study data stays in your browser. Objective Loop stores objectives, prompts, evidence links, review history, and preferences on this device.

What is sent over the network

The core app sends no study data, analytics, or tracking events. License purchase or verification contacts Sociobot’s billing API with a license token. Checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record.

Exports and deletion

Encrypted backups are created locally with your passphrase. We cannot recover it. Remove records in the app or clear this site’s browser storage.
